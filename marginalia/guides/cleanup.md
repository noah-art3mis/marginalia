# Clean up a draft

Use the brief to establish the permitted intervention. A cleanup pass can correct spelling, grammar, punctuation, and obvious sentence problems, but does not implicitly authorize restructuring the argument or changing its voice. Consult the optional style sheet for explicit consistency choices.

Preserve technical terms, names, numbers, citations, links, qualifications, and intentional fragments. Ask about ambiguous wording rather than turning a plausible interpretation into an asserted fact. Distinguish a convention the author chose from a preference you would impose.

Propose changes in manageable passages and explain material corrections. Keep original text untouched until acceptance. Declare substantive removals as information cuts even when they appear to simplify an awkward sentence. Follow `marginalia guide review` for the decision workflow; a rejection does not automatically become a style-sheet entry.
