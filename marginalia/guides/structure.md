# Propose headings and reorganize passages

Read the whole draft before suggesting an outline so passages near the end are considered. Use the author's request and draft to identify what the reader should understand; ask when that purpose is unclear. Lead with a short main narrative, then show the current and proposed heading trees as a before/after outline. Keep the first presentation focused on those trees; explain individual sections when requested.

Match proposed headings to the draft's register and the author's stated tone. A technical draft calls for technical headings; humor, irony, or a more dramatic investigative framing needs support in the brief or an author request. A request to change the headings' tone changes the outline, not the body text or a permanent style rule.

Obtain approval of the outline before applying it. Outline approval alone is not an instruction to move passages. When the author approves the outline and explicitly asks you to place the original paragraphs under it without rewriting, that instruction authorizes the approved heading changes and unambiguous moves. Record and apply those operations through Marginalia without asking again for each heading or move. Ask only about ambiguous placements, new editorial decisions, or changes outside that authorization.

Classify existing passages under approved headings without rewriting their body text. Preserve claims, examples, uncertainty, links, citations, punctuation, and wording. Do not summarize, deduplicate, translate, or quietly discard material during classification. Use an `insert` proposal with a unique existing passage as `original` and the heading plus separators as `replacement`; acceptance inserts the heading before the anchor and retains the anchor unchanged. Use replacement proposals for approved changes to existing headings.

Show each proposed move with its source, destination, and exact unchanged passage, in manageable batches when the author has authorized applying the structure. Submit moves through Marginalia rather than rebuilding the document with direct writes. For mixed paragraphs, discuss a split at existing sentence boundaries and preserve all original characters across the resulting passages. If the current CLI cannot express a split safely, leave the text unchanged and explain the limitation.

Ask an author query when a passage fits several sections or its intended role is unclear. Work through manageable portions. After accepted moves, inspect the resulting document and verify that every original body passage remains present. A content-preserving move can still be editorially misplaced, so mechanical text checks do not replace review of the organization.

Once the authorized reorganization is complete and inspected, finish the structuring pass through Versions. Start prose editing as a separate pass so reorganizing text does not silently become rewriting it.
