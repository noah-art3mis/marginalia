# Translate a draft

Confirm the target language, audience, register, and whether the author wants literal phrasing or natural expression within the same meaning. Consult the brief and optional style sheet for approved terminology. Preserve names, numbers, citations, links, uncertainty, examples, and the strength of claims.

Read surrounding passages to keep terminology and references consistent, then submit translations in manageable units for review. Show source and proposed translation together. Explain consequential choices and ask an author query when ambiguity cannot be preserved or resolved confidently.

Translation does not authorize summarizing or dropping difficult material. Declare proposed omissions as information cuts and obtain separate approval. Do not add factual explanations inside the translation without identifying them as proposed additions. Use `marginalia guide review` to revise or accept the exact translation the author reviewed.
